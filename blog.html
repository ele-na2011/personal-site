starting a nonprofit

august 1st, 2026

today i ran the first meeting for my nonprofit organization; sidequest. it was great seeing so many students interested in leadership and working to make a difference. but it also got me thinking. since when was making the world a better place so much the responsibility of youth, who are supposed to be enjoying the last years before they have to worry about incomes and taxes? it seems like the world right now is running on a system: adults create problems, youth attempt to solve them. adults create climate change, youth work their butts off trying to reverse it. i dont believe we should be handed everything on a silver platter, but i do believe that solving these problems should not be the entire responsibility of youth, under a title of initiative and getting into university. i believe that humanity's future is something everyone is responsible for, and youth should not be forced to give up their most innocent years to fix a world that is already broken beyond repair.

farming idea

july 30th, 2026

in grade 8 i once had to do a project; solve a SDG (sustainable development goal) in my own way. i pitched layered farming, which got overriden by a music project that helps dementia patients. looking back, there was definitely a solution to layered farming. with the amazon rainforest losing space and wildlife all around the world losing their homes, it would be a true lifesaver if humans were able to invent layered farms that occupy less horizontal space. however, the biggest problem i see with that is that sunlight may not be able to penetrate all the layers of plants. for this i see two solutions. mirrors and lenses that amplify the sunlight, or a layering strategy where the plants that need the most sunlight are at the top and the crops that need the least are at the bottom. should i make a 3d model of this?
